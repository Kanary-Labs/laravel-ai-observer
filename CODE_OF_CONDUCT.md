# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We want participation in AI Observatory to be welcoming, respectful, and
productive. Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Giving constructive, specific technical feedback.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding the disclosure of sensitive trace data.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and correcting mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions
that violate these standards. They may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[hello@kanarylabs.com](mailto:hello@kanarylabs.com). Reports will be reviewed
fairly and kept confidential as far as reasonably possible.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through the conduct-reporting channel.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project or Kanary Labs community.
